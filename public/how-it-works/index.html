How it works

One fiber. Many applications.

FiberVM is CoFiber’s sensing infrastructure platform. Follow the journey from light in a fiber to useful data for operators, developers, and researchers.

This overview follows the same journey as the interactive story.

01 · Fiber sensing

An interrogator sends pulses of light along an optical fiber. Changes in the returning light reveal vibrations and their position along the route.

02 · Shared infrastructure

FiberVM brings access and acquisition into a shared infrastructure layer, delivering data at different resolutions to multiple applications.

03 · The tenant experience

Tenants explore their data streams through a waterfall view, a route map, and channel analysis. This demonstration uses synthetic data.
